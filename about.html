About RealtySphere

RealtySphere is an independent real estate blog. It publishes research notes and guides on residential projects, locations, home buying, property investment, market trends, and RERA and documentation.

How we work

  • We explain what to check and where to verify it.
  • We do not publish prices, approvals or registration numbers we cannot source.
  • We date every article and show when it was last updated.

What we are not

RealtySphere is not a broker, developer or law firm, and does not give legal, financial or investment advice.
